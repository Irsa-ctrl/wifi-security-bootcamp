Lesson 1: Public Wi-Fi Risks

▼

What is the risk?

Public Wi-Fi networks are often unsecured, meaning anyone on the same network can potentially see your data.

Key Risks:

  • 🔴 Data Theft: Hackers can steal passwords, emails, and banking info
  • 🔴 Evil Twin Attacks: Fake networks that look like legitimate ones
  • 🔴 Man-in-the-Middle: Attackers intercept your communication
  • 🔴 Malware Distribution: Hackers can push malware to your device

How to Stay Safe:

  • ✅ Always use a VPN
  • ✅ Verify the network name with staff
  • ✅ Avoid banking on public Wi-Fi
  • ✅ Turn off auto-connect
🎣

Lesson 2: Evil Twin Attacks

▼

What is an Evil Twin?

An Evil Twin is a fake Wi-Fi network that mimics a legitimate one. It has the same name (SSID) but is controlled by an attacker.

How it works:

  • 1. Attacker creates a fake network with the same name as a real one
  • 2. Users connect to the fake network thinking it's real
  • 3. Attacker captures all data passing through
  • 4. Attacker can steal passwords, emails, and more

How to Detect:

  • ✅ Look for duplicate SSIDs in your network list
  • ✅ Check if the network has proper encryption
  • ✅ Verify with staff before connecting
  • ✅ Use our dashboard's duplicate SSID detection
🔑

Lesson 3: Password Security

▼

Why Password Security Matters?

Weak passwords are the #1 cause of data breaches. A strong password can protect your accounts from unauthorized access.

Characteristics of a Strong Password:

  • ✅ At least 12 characters long
  • ✅ Contains uppercase and lowercase letters
  • ✅ Contains numbers
  • ✅ Contains special characters (!@#$%^&*)
  • ✅ Doesn't contain common words or patterns
  • ✅ Is unique for each account

Password Best Practices:

  • ✅ Use a password manager
  • ✅ Enable 2-factor authentication
  • ✅ Never reuse passwords
  • ✅ Change passwords regularly
🛡️

Lesson 4: VPN Usage

▼

What is a VPN?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) encrypts your internet traffic and hides your IP address, making it safe to use public Wi-Fi.

Benefits of VPN:

  • ✅ Encrypts all your data
  • ✅ Hides your real IP address
  • ✅ Protects against hackers
  • ✅ Bypasses geo-restrictions

Choosing a VPN:

  • ✅ Use reputable providers (NordVPN, ExpressVPN)
  • ✅ Avoid free VPNs (they may sell your data)
  • ✅ Check for no-logs policy
  • ✅ Look for kill switch feature
📱

Lesson 5: Device Security

▼

Why Device Security Matters?

Your device is the gateway to your digital life. If it's compromised, all your data is at risk.

Device Security Tips:

  • ✅ Keep your OS updated
  • ✅ Enable device encryption
  • ✅ Use screen lock (PIN/Biometric)
  • ✅ Install security apps
  • ✅ Back up important data
  • ✅ Don't click suspicious links
  • ✅ Disable Bluetooth when not in use

Signs of Compromise:

  • ⚠️ Unusual battery drain
  • ⚠️ Unexpected pop-ups
  • ⚠️ Slow performance
  • ⚠️ Unknown apps installed
🌐

Lesson 6: Safe Browsing

▼

Safe Browsing Practices:

  • ✅ Look for HTTPS (padlock icon) in URL bar
  • ✅ Don't ignore browser security warnings
  • ✅ Keep your browser updated
  • ✅ Use ad-blockers and anti-tracking extensions
  • ✅ Clear cookies and cache regularly
  • ✅ Don't save passwords in browser on public devices

How to Identify Phishing Sites:

  • ⚠️ Check the URL carefully (misspellings)
  • ⚠️ Look for poor grammar and spelling
  • ⚠️ Beware of urgent requests
  • ⚠️ Don't click on suspicious links
